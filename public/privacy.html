Privacy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Machine Literate builds free AI lessons for students in grades 5–12. We are Machine Literate, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our EIN is 43-2331175.

Our mailing address is 845 United Nations Plaza, Apt 10A, New York, NY 10017.

What we collect

If you join our waitlist, we save your email address and whether you are a parent, teacher, student or other visitor. For students, we also save whether they are under 13 or 13+.

Students under 13 are asked to have a parent or guardian enter the parent’s email instead of their own. We also save whether the email is marked as a parent’s or the visitor’s own, a signup number and the date and time of signup.

We use waitlist details only to tell people when lessons open. We don’t sell them or use them for ads.

How we measure visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views without cookies. Google Analytics also starts in a mode that does not set cookies. It uses analytics cookies only if you click Allow in our cookie banner.

These tools help us learn which pages people visit and how many people join the waitlist. Our signup event sends Google only the role selected, such as parent or teacher. We do not send names or email addresses to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics event data is kept for 2 months. Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off, and we never allow advertising cookies.

You can read Google’s privacy policy to learn more.

No ads

There are no ads on this site.

Your choices

Allow and No thanks are equally available. We save your choice in your browser’s local storage, not in a cookie. You can use Cookie settings in any page’s footer to change it. Opening Cookie settings stops analytics cookies and deletes existing Google Analytics cookies; they stay off unless you click Allow again. Choosing No thanks keeps cookieless measurement on.

Email volunteer@machineliterate.org to ask to see or delete your waitlist info. You can also ask us to remove a child’s info.
